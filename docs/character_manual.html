Character Builder Reference Manual

Savage Worlds–style Star Wars character creation. Tables below list every option loaded from the project data/ folder.

Regenerate this file after editing JSON: python scripts/generate_character_manual.py

How to use this manual

  • Step 1 — Concept: Choose name, species, and career (see tables below).
  • Step 2 — Hindrances: Up to 4 hindrance points total (Minor = 1, Major = 2). Points pay for higher attributes, extra skills, and edges.
  • Step 3 — Attributes: Agility, Smarts, Spirit, Strength, Vigor (dice d4–d12).
  • Step 4 — Skills: Train skills; core skills start at d4.
  • Step 5 — Edges: Special advantages; Humans get one free edge.
  • Step 6 — Equipment: Spend starting credits on weapons, one armor, and gear.

Step 1 — Careers

Bounty Hunter

Licensed or freelance retrieval specialist. You get paid when the target is in cuffs—or a slab.

Benefit titleTypeEffect
Dead or alive paperworknarrativeYou can always identify local bounty-posting customs and typical bond rules without a roll (who pays, who enforces, what voids a claim).
Pressure at rangesituational+1 to Intimidation against a single target who knows you are armed and serious (once per scene per target).
Hunter’s marksituationalIn the first round of combat, once per combat, shoot at a target that has not acted this round; each raise adds 1d6 to damage.
Guild hall tonesituational+1 to Persuasion or Streetwise (pick each roll) when dealing with organizations that routinely work with hunters: bounty guilds, posting authorities, law-enforcement liaisons, or prison and transport contractors.

Colonist

Frontier settler, ag worker, or town pillar. You know how to make a life where supply lines are thin.

Benefit titleTypeEffect
Community facesituational+1 to Persuasion when dealing with locals in a settlement of ≤ 5,000 people.
Practical medicinesituational+1 to Healing when treating shock, heatstroke, dehydration, or vermin bites—not lightsaber wounds or major surgery.

Co-op voicesituational+1 to Persuasion or Streetwise (pick each roll) when dealing with organizations rooted in settlement life: homestead councils, agricultural co-ops, colonial administration, or frontier relief and supply leagues.

Hired Gun

Mercenary, enforcer, or line soldier for credits. Violence is the job description.

Merc's codesituational+1 to Persuasion or Streetwise (pick each roll) when dealing with organizations that trade in force: mercenary companies, PMC contracting desks, arms suppliers, or veteran and muster societies.

Politico

Diplomat, public advocate, or deal-broker who wins conflicts before blasters clear leather.

Benefit titleTypeEffect
Floor presencesituational+1 to Persuasion when addressing a group, hearing, council, or negotiation table where your target can hear your full argument.
Biting retortsituationalOnce per combat, as a free action, make a Taunt test against one target who can hear and understand you; on a success, the target is Distracted until the end of its next turn, and on a raise it is also Vulnerable.

Market briefsituational+1 to Persuasion or Streetwise (pick each roll) when dealing with organizations built on policy and commerce: trade delegations, customs boards, colonial councils, or corporate affairs offices.

Smuggler

Blockade sprinter, fixer, or fast-talking loader. Cargo flies if the story holds.

Benefit titleTypeEffect
Gray manifestsituational+1 to Thievery or Streetwise when hiding contraband in a vehicle, crate, or false panel you prepared at least one scene in advance.
Yeah, I bet you have...situationalOnce per scene, if you are in a conversation with the target, you may attempt to draw your blaster and attack. Roll Thievery; on a success, you have the Drop on that target for the attack.
Fixer’s introductionnarrativeIn any starport Class III or larger, you may know a fence, loader’s union rep, or corrupt clerk by reputation; the GM names them when you arrive.
Gray channelsituational+1 to Persuasion or Streetwise (pick each roll) when dealing with organizations that routinely touch the shadow trade: fences, blockade-runner rings, dockside syndicates, or black-market clearing houses.

Technician

Ship’s engineer, slicer, or grease-stained genius. If it has wires, you’ve sworn at it professionally.

Benefit titleTypeEffect
Battlefield patchsituational+1 to Repair when fixing something damaged in the last hour during combat or an active chase.

Found a sparegear_accessOnce per scene, make a Repair roll (0 to 4 penalty, GM discretion) on a technical item; on a success, you have a spare of that item. If you fail, you damage the item until it is repaired.
Yard credsituational+1 to Persuasion or Streetwise (pick each roll) when dealing with organizations that keep ships and machines running: shipyards, mechanics’ unions, factory service desks, or certified repair and parts networks.
