Concept & species

Hindrances

Up to 4 hindrance points (Major = 2, Minor = 1). Selections feed attributes, skills, and edges.

Attributes

Each attribute starts at d4. You have 5 die steps; extra steps cost 2 hindrance points each.

Skills

Skill budget is one pool: 15 creation points plus hindrance points left after attributes. Raising skills spends from that pool only. Any skill cost over 15 is also tagged to hindrance so the panel can show how much hindrance is still free for Edges—it is not a second charge on top of “skill budget remaining.”

Edges

Each edge costs 2 hindrance points after skills. If your species is Human, a free-edge choice appears below.

Weapons, armor & gear

Credits depend on hindrances (e.g. Poverty). Purchases cannot exceed starting credits.
